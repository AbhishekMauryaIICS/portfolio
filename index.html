About Me

A bit about how I think, build and work across engineering, security and growth.

Hi, I'm Abhishek Maurya — a multi-disciplinary tech professional with 8+ years of hands-on experience across software development, web engineering, cybersecurity and digital marketing. Currently I'm working at IICS Computer Education, Lajpat Nagar Branch, where I mentor students and build curriculum around real-world technology.

As a Software Developer & Full Stack Web Developer, I architect and ship end-to-end applications — from pixel-perfect frontends to robust backends and cloud deployments. I write clean, maintainable code and design APIs that teams are happy to work with, not around.

As a Cyber Security Expert, I approach every system I touch with an attacker's mindset — identifying vulnerabilities before they can be exploited. From penetration testing and OWASP audits to network hardening and secure code reviews, I make sure what gets shipped stays safe.

As a Digital Marketer, I help brands convert online presence into real revenue through data-driven SEO, Google & Meta Ads, and performance-first content strategies. I believe growth is a system — and systems can always be optimised.

I'm a continuous learner by nature — whether it's a new framework, a new exploit technique, or a new ad platform, I make it a habit to ship something I've learned every week.
